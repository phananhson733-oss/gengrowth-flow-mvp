## Sun Sign vs Rising Sign: Why One Holds and One Wobbles

greta lee zodiac sign sits on much firmer ground than the Rising sign attached to it, and the difference comes down to what each one needs to be calculated. The person-centered tradition Dane Rudhyar helped shape treats the Ascendant, Sun, and Moon as the chart's core triangle — but only two of those three can be trusted for Lee right now.

### Why the Sun Holds

A Sun sign needs only the date, so Lee's Pisces Sun stays steady no matter what else is missing. The Sun works by marking conscious identity and core motivation, the long-term direction a person grows toward, and you can read it confidently here because the input is fully public. This is the placement nearly every source agrees on, and it does the heaviest lifting in any honest read of her.

### Why the Rising Wobbles

A Rising sign — the subject of any good [[<TBD-internal-link: explainer on how the rising sign is calculated>]] — changes roughly every two hours, so it needs the exact minute of birth to be trustworthy. It works by setting the chart's entire house structure and the "first impression" layer a person leads with. The twelve-house system it anchors is commonly traced to Hellenistic astrology in the early centuries CE, and it has always depended on a precise time. Without that hour, any Ascendant published for Lee is a best guess dressed up as fact.

### The Trade-Off in Practice

Here the trade-off becomes concrete. To get a stable, defensible read you lean on the Sun alone, but you sacrifice the first-impression and house detail that only a timed chart can give. Reach instead for an unverified Rising and you gain a richer-looking story at the cost of accuracy. The cleaner choice for Lee is the first one — say less, but say it honestly.
