## Sun Sign vs Rising Sign: Why One Holds and One Wobbles

greta lee zodiac sign sits on far firmer ground than the Rising sign attached to it, and the difference is what each one needs to be calculated. The person-centered tradition Dane Rudhyar helped shape treats the Ascendant, Sun, and Moon as the chart's core triangle — but only the first two are trustworthy for Lee right now.

### Why the Sun Holds

A Sun sign needs only the date, so Lee's Pisces Sun stays steady no matter what else is missing. It marks conscious identity and core motivation — the long-term direction a person grows toward — and you can read it confidently here because the input is fully public. This is the placement nearly every source agrees on, and it does the heaviest lifting in any honest read of her.

### Why the Rising Wobbles

A Rising sign — the subject of any good [[<TBD-internal-link: explainer on how the rising sign is calculated>]] — changes roughly every two hours, so it needs the exact minute of birth. It sets the chart's entire house structure and the "first impression" layer a person leads with. Without that hour, any Ascendant published for Lee is a best guess dressed up as fact.

### The Trade-Off in Practice

The trade-off is concrete. For a stable, defensible read you lean on the Sun alone and give up the house and first-impression detail only a timed chart provides. Reach for an unverified Rising instead and you gain a richer-looking story at the cost of accuracy. For Lee the cleaner choice is the first — say less, but say it honestly.

Most misreadings come from treating an unfinished chart as a finished one. Three recur, and each is worth correcting on its own terms:
