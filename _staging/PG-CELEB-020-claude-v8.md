## Sun-Sign Labels vs a Full Natal Chart: What Actually Differs

Jaylen Brown birth chart readings split into two very different products, and confusing them is the most common trap. A Sun-sign label works by isolating one placement — the Scorpio Sun — and generalizing it to millions of people born across a month. A full natal chart works the other way: it reads the Scorpio Sun in context with the Moon, ascendant, and the angles planets make to each other, so the interpretation is specific to one person and one moment.

The trade-off is real in both directions. To get the speed and shareability of a Sun-sign label, you sacrifice accuracy — you end up with a caption that fits everyone and no one. To get the precision of a full chart, you sacrifice simplicity; synthesizing several placements takes time and a tolerance for nuance. Liz Greene's psychological approach leans hard into that second path, treating contradictions in a chart as the point rather than a bug. For a public figure, the full-chart read is what separates "intense Scorpio athlete" from a portrait that also accounts for the social-justice voice and the conviction behind it.
