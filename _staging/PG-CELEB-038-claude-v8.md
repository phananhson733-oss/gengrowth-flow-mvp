## Common Misreadings

Most confusion around the kai cenat zodiac sign comes from treating one sign as a full verdict. These are the misreads worth correcting:

1. **"His zodiac sign proves he's a nonstop extrovert."** A Sun sign describes tendencies, not a guarantee. The Capricorn side often placed in his chart can read as private, measured, and quietly disciplined.
2. **"The kai cenat zodiac sign is his entire personality."** The Sun is a single placement. Moon, Mercury, Venus, and Mars fill in the emotional, mental, and relational parts one sign can't cover.
3. **"Sagittarius means he can't be serious."** Expansive energy and hard graft coexist. The broader chart hints at both bold drive and real structure, which is why the biography-and-full-breakdown dumps miss the point.

Each of these swaps a symbolic lens for a deterministic claim—exactly the trap a focused Sun-sign read is meant to avoid.
