## Full Birth Chart vs Sun Sign: What Each Actually Shows

### How Each Read Works

A Sun-sign read starts and stops at Sagittarius. It works by taking one placement and describing broad themes — optimism, bluntness, a love of the big swing. It is quick and portable, which is why horoscope columns rely on it. A full-chart read works by layering every placement instead. The Sagittarius Sun gets tempered by Capricorn discipline, softened by a Libra Moon, and angled toward community by an Aquarius North Node. Psychological astrologers such as Liz Greene stress reading those layers as tendencies that interact, not as separate labels stacked in a list.
