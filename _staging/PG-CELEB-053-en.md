## Western Natal Chart vs Vedic Kundli: What Actually Differs

The Jeff Bezos birth chart shows up online in two very different forms, and readers conflate them constantly. One is the Western natal wheel — tropical zodiac, Sun in Capricorn, psychological interpretation. The other is a Vedic Kundli (a Jyotish chart), which uses the sidereal zodiac and often shifts his Sun back into Sagittarius, plus a Navamsa (D9) sub-chart Western astrology doesn't use.

Here is how it works in practice. Western reading leans on aspects between planets and the symbolic character of each sign to sketch personality and public arc. Vedic reading leans on dashas — planetary time periods — and house lords to describe timing and life circumstance. To get the psychological, character-first portrait Western astrology offers, you sacrifice the predictive timing structure that Vedic Kundli reports foreground. Choosing the Western wheel over the Kundli gets you a coherent story about who he is; you lose the sidereal chart's emphasis on when.

A third framing floats around search results — the "spirit animal / Life Path number" mashup — but that isn't astrology at all. It blends numerology and pop symbolism, so comparing it to either chart tradition is a category error rather than a real trade-off.
