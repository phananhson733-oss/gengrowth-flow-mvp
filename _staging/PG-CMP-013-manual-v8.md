## Common Misreadings When Comparing These Tools

1. **Reading the discount as the saving.** The 60% price cut arrives with doubled limits, so unit cost falls about 4.9x — and on expiry it rises by the same factor, not by the 2.5x the price alone implies. Budget the renewal at list rates and treat the promotional period as a bonus.
2. **Comparing prompt counts without dividing.** Scalenut Professional's 100 prompts at $199 and Clearscope Business's 300 at $399 look like a small gap on the sticker and a 1.5x gap per prompt. The counts and the prices have to move together or the comparison means nothing.
3. **Assuming the tracked models are the same.** Scalenut covers ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews at every tier and adds Perplexity only at $199; Clearscope's pages describe tracking across ChatGPT and Gemini; Frase gates Claude and Gemini behind its $239 tier. Where your buyers actually ask questions decides which coverage matters.
4. **Treating refresh rate as a detail.** Surfer refreshes 25 prompts weekly on Standard and 50 daily on Pro; Scalenut's tiers list weekly refresh throughout. A daily number and a weekly number are not the same measurement, whatever the per-prompt cost says.

## Common Questions About Scalenut Alternatives

**What is the cheapest Scalenut alternative?**

For prompt tracking specifically, NeuronWriter's Bronze plan at $23 plus its $9 add-on is the lowest entry outlay, and its add-on rate of $0.90 per question is the cheapest per-prompt figure in this comparison. For content volume, Autoblogging.ai starts at $19 on a credit meter.

**Is Scalenut's 60% discount worth taking?**

It is a real discount with real doubled limits, and the arithmetic is better than it looks — about 4.9x on unit cost. The catch is symmetrical: at renewal without the offer, unit cost rises by the same factor. Take it with the renewal budgeted at list.

**Which alternative tracks the most AI models?**

Scalenut adds Perplexity at $199; Frase reaches Claude and Gemini at $239; Clearscope's pages describe ChatGPT and Gemini; Surfer's plans list ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Gemini. Match the list against where your buyers ask, not against its length.

**Does Scalenut still do content writing?**

Yes — its tiers list article creation and optimization (5/30/75 a month at list, doubled on the promotion) alongside the prompt tracking. The pricing page simply leads with GEO now.

**Is there a free Scalenut alternative?**

Trials are common — Scalenut lists 7 days, Clearscope 14, NeuronWriter 7 with a 30-day first-purchase refund — but none of the tracking tools here lists a permanently free plan on its pricing page.

**Why do per-prompt costs vary so much?**

Because prompt tracking is bundled differently everywhere: sometimes as an entry-tier teaser, sometimes as the product itself, sometimes as an add-on. The label converged faster than the pricing did, which is exactly why dividing is worth the minute it takes.

**Do I need any of this if my pages are not being cited at all?**

Tracking tells you that you are not cited; it does not tell you why. If the pages are thin on structure or metadata, that is the cheaper thing to fix first, and it is the only job our agent does.
