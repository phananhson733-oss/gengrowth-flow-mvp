## Common Misreadings

When reading Harry Potter characters zodiac signs, a small set of errors comes up consistently. Here's where each tends to go wrong:

1. **Conflating Hogwarts house and zodiac sign.** Gryffindor is not a fire-sign filter. Hermione is a Virgo and a Gryffindor—those two facts coexist without contradiction. The Sorting Hat selects on qualities the books describe as courage, loyalty, ambition, and wit. These don't map to astrological elements.
2. **Substituting actor charts for character archetypes.** A performer's natal placements and Hermione Granger's Virgo reading are different reference points. Using the first to explain the second is a category error that muddies both readings without clarifying either.
3. **Assigning based on a single trait rather than the full pattern.** Calling Voldemort a Scorpio because he reads as dark and controlling misses the more precise Capricorn assignment. His arc is about institutional domination, the systematic elimination of everything outside his reach, and a pathological refusal to accept mortality. The Capricorn shadow is about ambition without empathy and control as a substitute for safety. A Scorpio archetype transforms through the encounter with mortality; Voldemort attempts to eliminate it from the equation entirely—which is the Capricorn response to the one limit that institutional power cannot reach.
4. **Demanding determinism from an interpretive framework.** Saying Hermione "is a Virgo" means the Virgo archetype fits her patterns more precisely than any other sign does. It does not mean fictional characters carry functioning natal charts. The framework is symbolic and interpretive, not predictive.
