## Common Misreadings

Several shortcuts make celebrity-chart posts less useful than they appear.

1. **“Pisces explains everything about Suga.”** A Sun sign is one symbolic layer, not a complete account of a person’s history, craft, relationships, or choices.
2. **“A published chart wheel proves the houses.”** A wheel can be generated with a default time. Without a verified time source, its houses remain provisional.
3. **“A fan-assigned rising sign is established data.”** Rising signs require the time of birth. A confident claim should name a reliable source for that time.
4. **“Moon details are always date-only facts.”** The Moon moves quickly enough that time and degree matter, especially when a page claims precision.
5. **“Symbolism is biography.”** A symbolic reading can invite reflection, but it cannot establish motives or private facts about a public figure.
