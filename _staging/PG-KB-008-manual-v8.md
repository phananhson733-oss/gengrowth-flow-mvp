## Common Misreadings

Several shortcuts can make a public-figure chart sound more certain than the available data permits.

1. **“Virgo explains RM completely.”** A Sun sign is one symbolic category, not an account of an individual’s history, relationships, choices, or work.
2. **“A displayed Ascendant is verified.”** The Ascendant needs a time and location. A default-time calculation is not a documented birth record.
3. **“The Moon is automatically date-only.”** The Moon moves quickly enough that time, degree, and location can matter when a claim is precise.
4. **“A fan-made chart is a primary source.”** A graphic is a calculation or interpretation unless it cites the underlying birth-data source.
5. **“Symbolic language proves biography.”** Symbolism can support reflection, but it does not establish private facts or causal explanations.
