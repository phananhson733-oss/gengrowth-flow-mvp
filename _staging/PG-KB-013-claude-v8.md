## Common Misreadings

BLACKPINK zodiac signs gets extended in ways that push past what the framework actually supports. Four patterns come up most often:

1. **Treating the sun sign as the whole chart.** Sun-sign reading describes one layer of a person's astrological picture. Calling Jisoo "a typical Capricorn" based solely on her birthday flattens everything the rest of her chart might add — moon sign, rising sign, dominant aspects — into a single archetype label. The sun sign is a starting point, not a summary.
2. **Importing unverified birth time data.** Fan sites frequently list birth times for BLACKPINK members without an official source. Any moon sign or rising sign reading built on that data is speculation, not chart analysis. BLACKPINK zodiac signs stays within the confirmed layer, which is why it remains a defensible starting point even for readers who are skeptical of astrology in general.
3. **Extrapolating intensity from archetype association.** Capricorn's association with ambition doesn't mean Jisoo or Jennie are more ambitious than Rosé or Lisa. The archetype describes the form that energy takes, not how much of it is present or how strongly it drives behavior in any given moment.
4. **Expecting cross-context consistency.** A member's off-camera personality, interview affect, and stage presence may each read differently from the same sun-sign archetype. Context shapes expression; the label doesn't predict every situation or override individual variation.
