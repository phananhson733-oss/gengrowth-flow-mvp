## Common Misreadings

Several familiar shortcuts make celebrity astrology less useful than it first appears.

1. **“Capricorn explains Jisoo.”** A Sun sign is one symbolic layer, not a complete account of a person’s relationships, history, public role, or creative decisions.
2. **“A chart graphic confirms the houses.”** Houses depend on a birth time and location; a default-time wheel is not a verified record.
3. **“The Moon is a fixed date-only detail.”** Detailed lunar claims can be time-sensitive, particularly when a page adds degree or house information.
4. **“A fan consensus is a source.”** Repeated claims can still rest on the same unsourced assumption.
5. **“Symbolism is proof.”** Astrological language can invite reflection without demonstrating a biographical fact.
