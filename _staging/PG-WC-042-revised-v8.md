## Sun Sign vs Full Birth Chart: What the Missing Hour Leaves Out

A Sun sign is one fact: on July 13, 2007, the Sun was in Cancer, and that holds no matter what time he was born. A full natal chart adds houses, a rising sign, and angular emphasis — and every one of those needs a verified birth time. The Sun sign is rock-solid but broad; it points to core temperament and says little about how that temperament gets channeled. The full chart trades certainty for specificity: the exact minute fixes the houses and the ascendant, turning a general profile into a pointed one.

Without the hour, that specificity simply isn't available for him. This is also why competing sites disagree: each plugs in a different guessed time, so their house-based readings split even though the planets themselves match. The date-driven placements are the shared, solid ground; everything time-driven is where the versions diverge.
