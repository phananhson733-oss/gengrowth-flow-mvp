## Tropical Chart vs Draconic Chart: Where the Readings Split

The Lamine Yamal birth chart shows up online in more than one flavor, and the two most confused are the tropical natal chart and the draconic chart. The tropical chart is the standard Western reading: it maps the planets against the seasonal zodiac, so his Sun lands in Cancer and drives the familiar water-sign read of protective instinct and emotional feel for the game. A draconic chart re-anchors every placement to the Moon's north node, producing a second, more inward layer that some astrologers interpret alongside the tropical one.

Here is how each works and where the trade-off sits. The tropical chart functions as the grounded, checkable frame — same date, same placements, repeatable for any 2007-born teammate. The draconic overlay reads for deeper motivation but floats further from anything you can observe on the pitch. To get the symbolic depth of a draconic read, you sacrifice the plain, testable footing the tropical chart gives you. A third option, the Chinese Purple Star reading, answers a different question entirely on a separate zodiac, so stacking it onto the tropical chart only creates contradictions.
