Outcome prediction runs the opposite way: it tries to collapse those same rich symbols into a binary — win or lose — that astrology's own interpretive tradition never promised to deliver.

That difference in how it works is not a small technicality. One approach stays inside what the symbols can honestly carry; the other borrows the authority of astrology to make a claim the practice was never built to make.

## Common Misreadings

Most quick takes on this topic stumble in the same handful of places, usually because they want the charts to do something the charts can't. Naming the misreadings keeps the whole exercise honest and stops it drifting into fortune-telling.

1. **"The charts predict the winner."** They don't. This reading describes temperament and timing, and it makes no claim on the final score.
2. **"A stronger chart means a stronger team."** There is no cosmic league table. A chart is a character sketch, not a power rating you can rank.
3. **"One transit decides the match."** A transit sets a mood over a window of time; it can't override form, tactics, or a shot that clips a defender and loops in.
4. **"National astrology is destiny."** A country's chart is a symbolic portrait, not a fixed fate its players are somehow locked into.

Keep those four in view and the reading stays where it belongs: an interpretive lens for enjoying the fixture, never a substitute for the match itself.
