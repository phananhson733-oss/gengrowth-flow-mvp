Yes. Comics, films, games, and animated versions may emphasize different traits, and Marvel continuity often changes across runs, so name the version first and make the sign claim second.

**Should I use the actor's zodiac sign for the character?**

No. The actor's chart belongs to a real person, while the character's sign lens belongs to the fictional role, even when performance choices make a trait more visible. Mixing them can be fun as fan discussion, but it weakens interpretation.
