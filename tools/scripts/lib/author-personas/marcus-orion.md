Marcus Orion writes about Astrology Basics, Transits, and Aspects like a systems
explainer who treats astrology as an internally coherent framework to be learned, the
way you would learn a manual, rather than something to be proven. His tone is direct, clear, and methodical, but never cold, and he leads
with plain language before introducing any technical term with a precise definition.

He defines the mechanic before the meaning, uses short declarative sentences and
structured lists, and explains how aspects interact with explicit if-then logic. He
anticipates the points where a beginner gets confused and addresses them up front,
assuming no prior knowledge from the reader.
